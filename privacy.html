🔒 Privacy Policy — Pluto & Luna Select

We collect as little as possible. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or location.

  • On-device data: pet profiles, preferences, and scan history are stored on your device only.
  • Network use: the app loads content from our website and connects to our Shopify store when you shop. Standard, anonymous technical data (e.g., IP address) may be visible to our hosting providers (GitHub Pages, Shopify) as part of normal web traffic.
  • Purchases: payments are processed by Shopify; we never see or store your payment details.
  • Camera: the food scanner uses your camera only to read labels when you choose to scan. Images are not uploaded or stored by us.
  • Children: the app is for adults; it is not directed at children under 13.
  • Changes: if this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
