How scoring works

  • Named animal protein first = good. Vague "meat meal" = not good.
  • Artificial colors, flavors & preservatives lower the score.
  • Xylitol (birch sugar) = instant avoid + toxicity alert.
  • AAFCO nutritional adequacy statement = verified completeness.
  • Every point is explained — the "why" list cites the exact ingredient.
  • Your dog's conditions (allergies, kidney disease…) personalize every result.
Not veterinary advice. The scanner is a shopping aid, not a diagnosis. Final food decisions belong to you and your vet.
