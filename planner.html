How it will work

  1. Pick your dog — weight, age, activity, conditions set the targets (AAFCO/NRC profiles).
  2. Choose ingredients — from USDA-verified foods you actually buy.
  3. We balance it — protein, fat, calcium:phosphorus, essential vitamins & minerals, with supplement gaps flagged.
  4. Vet-nutritionist reviewed — every plan template passes professional review before it reaches you.

Why this matters

95% of published home-prepared dog recipes are deficient in at least one essential nutrient (Stockman et al., JAVMA 2013). Good intentions aren't nutrition — math is. That's what this planner does.
